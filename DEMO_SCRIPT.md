# SentinelAI • 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

---

### ⏱️ 0:00 – 0:20 | Problem

*"Hi judges! Traditional technical interviews are broken. Static screening tools ask rigid, one-size-fits-all questions that ignore a candidate's background. On the other hand, generic AI chatbots lose track of context, repeat questions, hallucinate achievements, or get bypassed with simple prompt injections. Hiring teams need an intelligent interviewer that evaluates technical depth reliably."*

---

### ⏱️ 0:20 – 0:45 | Introducing SentinelAI

*"Meet SentinelAI — an adaptive, candidate-aware AI Technical Interviewer. SentinelAI combines deterministic strategy control with Groq LLM natural language processing. It analyzes a candidate's background signals, selects personalized curriculum topics, enforces strict interview invariants, and generates evidence-grounded final debrief reports."*

---

### ⏱️ 0:45 – 1:30 | Live Adaptive Interview

*"Let's select Alex Turner, a Backend Software Engineer candidate. As soon as I click 'Start Interview', SentinelAI initializes a isolated SQLite session state. Notice Question 1: SentinelAI automatically selected 'Full-Stack Integration & Streaming Responses' because it matches Alex's backend engineering profile.*

*Now I'll submit a detailed technical answer explaining Server-Sent Events (SSE), backpressure management, and client reconnection strategies."*

---

### ⏱️ 1:30 – 2:00 | Technical Depth & Adaptive Probing

*"Watch what happens next: SentinelAI evaluates technical accuracy, depth, practical reasoning, and communication. Because Alex provided a strong answer, SentinelAI's strategy engine triggers an adaptive deep dive into concurrency and cancellation trade-offs.*

*Look at the Interview Map: as competencies are assessed, topics move seamlessly from UPCOMING to ACTIVE to COVERED."*

---

### 2:00 – 2:30 | Evidence-Based Assessment Debrief

*"When the interview reaches its completion criteria — minimum 8 questions across at least 4 curriculum domains — SentinelAI generates a final assessment debrief.*

*Unlike generic AI templates, every strength bullet is strictly formatted as [Competency]: [Evidence Demonstrated]. Notice how strengths detail exact demonstrated concepts like SSE streaming, backpressure, and vector retrieval, while areas to strengthen map directly to actionable next steps."*

---

### 2:30 – 3:00 | Architecture & Scalability

*"Under the hood, SentinelAI runs Node.js, Express, SQLite persistence, and Groq Llama 3 LLM acceleration. Our state machine enforces 100% deterministic completion logic — prompt injections cannot skip questions or force early passes. If Groq experiences rate limits or timeouts, our fallback evaluators guarantee zero UI downtime.*

*Thank you! We'd love to take your questions."*
